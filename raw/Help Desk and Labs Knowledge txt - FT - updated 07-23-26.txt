" KB0014360 FT FT - Connect - PowerShell Cmdlets "
To connect to PowerShell, follow the documentation provided by Connect Support. This page also contains the full list of available cmdlets which can be used, with some contained below for reference which can be used on our internal groups to adjust permissions as indicated by ACE.
Group Commands
Get-DistributionGroupMember -identity <GroupName> gives all members of a distribution group.
Add-DistributionGroupMember -identity <GroupName> -member <Member> adds the member to the group.
Remove-DistributionGroupMember -identity <GroupName> -member <Member> removes the member from the group.
Get-UnifiedGroupLinks -identity <GroupName> gives all members of a unified group.
Add-UnifiedGroupLinks -identity <GroupName> -Link Type <Owner/Member> -Links <Member,Member> adds the member(s) to the group.
Remove-UnifiedGroupLinks -identity <GroupName> -Link Type <Owner/Member> -Links <Member,Member> removes the member(s) to the group.
SendAS
Get-RecipientPermission -identity <Group> -AccessRights SendAS lists all with sendas permissions on a desired group.
Add-RecipientPermission -identity <Group> -AccessRights SendAS -Trustee <Member> adds sendas permissions for the desired member on a group.
Remove-RecipientPermission -identity <Group> -AccessRights SendAS -Trustee <Member> removes sendas permissions for the desired member on a group.

" KB0014165 FT FT - Using REGIS for the Help Desk "
Accessing REGIS
1. Go to https://sims.rutgers.edu/rosters/ .
2. Click the Rosters & Electronic Grading Information System (REGIS) link.
3. Log in through Central Authentication (CAS).
Navigating REGIS
There are 4 tabs available.
View Rosters
There are two tabs available: Quick Search and Drill-down Search.
Quick Search
1. Quick Search requires that you know the full course number. Everything but the section number is required.
2. Then click the Retrieve Roster button.
3. The class roster will be displayed. See the FERPA warning below regarding the proper use of this information.
4. We have access to see all courses. Faculty members will be restricted to those courses for which they have been given permission by the department chair or his/her designee.
5. Click the View Rosters tab to return to the main View Rosters page.
Drill-down Search
1. Drill-down Search allows you to search for course information.
2. Use pulldown menus to select criteria.
3. Click Retrieve Roster
View Roster Status
1. Enter course number and click View Status button.
2. Roster status and the assigned professor is displayed.
View & Grant/Revoke Privileges
1. The View & Grant/Revoke Privileges page shows a person's current privileges. It shows your own privileges by default, and only for the current and previous years.
2. Older sets of privileges are found through the Display Complete List ... link.
3. Searches for others can be done by name or NetID.
4. Click on a name to see its privs.
5. Help Desk staff cannot change privs. We can only see the current ones. There are four main types of privs, each with its own set of abilities. 
o Someone who is unable to do something in REGIS may not have been granted the privs for that access. Refer the person to either the person who granted the existing or previous privs (shown in the "Granted By" column) or to the departmental administrator (if no privs have been granted, or the previous granter is not available).
6. We have also found undefined DEAN and GC_GUEST roles/privs.
Main Menu
Returns you to the page with the Rosters & Electronic Grading Information System (REGIS) link.
How to use REGIS access
1. For people with existing access, see who granted the access and send the inquirer to that person.
2. For people who never had access before, or in cases where the original granter is no longer around, refer the inquirer to the department chair or administrator.
3. We cannot search to see who has access to a given class roster. We can only search for access via a given name or a NetID that has access.
4. Dos/Don'ts 
o This tool is intended for use in specific cases to research someone's reported REGIS problem. Abuse of this access will result in immediate termination. Examples of abuse might be a parent searching for a child's roster or an idle examination of a colleague's roster.
o Pay attention to the FERPA restrictions. They are briefly described in every displayed roster.
o Rosters and privileges may also be viewed for REGIS training purposes.
o Do not look up info on behalf of one of our employees. Refer the person to his/her professor.
o Every time you use it, please make sure you are making a comment in the relevant ServiceNow incident. Document what you did and why you did it. Avoid including specific people's info in the ticket.
o Log out of the REGIS tool when done; do not leave REGIS windows active. Delete any downloaded data when done.
o When someone asks about a REGIS problem, you may tell them, "I need to verify your identity to make sure you have the right to this information." Do not show REGIS information to anyone other than the person whose access you are checking. (For example, do not tell a TA what sort of access the professor has.)
o Refer all access/priv problems to the granter or department. You may explain that we cannot make changes in REGIS.

" KB0017889 IT Help Desk - Application Support ScarletMail Programmable Search Engine Request "
In the Slack channel #scarlet-support-staff, or your SCTASKs, you may see a RITM from ScarletMail Support requesting that the Help Desk confirm specific information regarding a ScarletMail Programmable Search Engine request.
Google Programmable Search Engine (PSE), formerly known as Google Custom Search Engine (CSE), lets web developers add a customizable search box to their web pages. The search uses the base Google search engine; however, it can be customized, for example, to only search specific sites. This product is popular for compliance with Rutgers website policy 80.1.7 "Search Function" requirements. A ScarletMail PSE, which may be requested via this form, offers benefits such as OIT-administered ownership that survives unit-level personnel changes and excludes advertising.
To handle the request:
1. When you open the RITM, scroll down to the SCTASKs and open the one assigned to OIT-HD - ScarletMail Support Staff.
2. Assign the task to yourself. If you cannot assign the task to yourself, please inform a manager.
3. The SCTASK will ask you to confirm the following information: 
o Confirm the status/authority of the listed DDDC. DDDC refers to the Requestor Dean/Director/Chair. Job titles can be checked in CAT or IDCheck.
o Verify University business affiliation. Is the requester a Rutgers University department member?
o Verify 18+ status (whether through self-affirmation and/or IdM records). Verify that the requester is 18 or older.
4. Once all this information is confirmed, state this in a work note and change the state of the SCTASK to "Closed Complete".
5. If this request came through Slack or email, respond when the task is set as Closed Complete.
See RITM0064214 as an example of the request and process.

o Answer Thank you for calling the Rutgers IT Staff Direct line, my name is [NAME]. May I please have the spelling of your NetID?

* If the issue is more technical consider transferring to New Brunswick Level 2 queue otherwise send to New Brunswick General.

* ResNet Tools we have access to:
* Fluke 
o Used to test wall and switch ports for functionality, cable and switch information
o Can diagnose cable issues
* Loopback 
o Used to send fluke signal back through wire pairs to fully diagnose cable issues in wall cabling
o Locate user port on patch panel
* Network Support Laptop 
o Used to comment in tickets, research information, wireless surveys, and troubleshoot 802.1x ResNet issues
* Wired NIC 
o Test 802.1x on devices without native wired NICs and to diagnose NIC issues
* Wireless NIC 
o For survey standardization and testing of user devices to diagnose NIC issues
* Test Router 
o To test if a device which is failing to authenticate on ResNet can authenticate behind a MAB d router instead
* ToolsUSB 
o Holds useful software (i.e. AV, RUW_S installers, and Network Support software)
* Hub 
o Simple packet collection during Wireshark analysis for NOC escalation, this will be explained in more detail in a successive section
* Patch cable (Ethernet cable) 
o To replace damaged patch cables, and to test in place of a user cable
o Cables on the back wall are all tested good cables
* Teleco Closet Keys 
o Keys to all building telecom (switch) closets
* BCM 
o Key which opens some telecom doors which are not opened by standard building keys
* Campus Master 
o Master keys for each campus in the event that the building key does not open the door or does not exist
* 2AA Master 
o Shared key between HD and NOC that should open all telecom doors on campus
o Only CSq can get this key from the safe and it should only be used if necessary per the Switchlist/Keylist or other keys previously did not open the teleco door
